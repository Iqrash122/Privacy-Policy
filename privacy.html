01

Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and published by Swift Sculpt on the Google Play Store, now and in the future. By downloading or using any Swift Sculpt app, you agree to the practices described in this document.

One policy for all apps. Every app we publish — regardless of its category or purpose — is covered under this single policy. If a future app requires its own separate policy due to unique data practices, that will be clearly stated on that app's Play Store listing and within the app itself.

Where an app collects data that goes beyond what is described here, that app will display an additional in-app notice explaining the difference before collecting any such data.

02

Information We Collect

Our principle: We collect only the minimum data needed for the app to function. We never collect data in the background or for purposes beyond what is described here.

Information you give us directly

  • Account information such as your name and email address when you register
  • Profile details and preferences you set up within an app
  • Content you create inside the app (documents, records, entries, or similar)
  • Messages or details you send when contacting our support

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version for compatibility and bug fixing
  • Crash reports and error logs to help us fix technical issues — these are anonymized
  • Aggregated, anonymous usage patterns (which features are used most) to help us improve the app

Information collected only with your permission

  • Location data — only when you actively use a location-based feature within the app
  • Camera or microphone — only when you initiate a feature that requires it
  • Contacts — only when you choose to import data from your contact list
  • Biometric data — processed entirely on your device by your phone's own operating system; we never receive or store biometric information
03

How We Use Your Data

We use collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To operate and deliver the app's core features
  • To sync your data securely across your devices where cloud sync is offered
  • To send push notifications, reminders, or alerts — only when you have enabled them
  • To identify, investigate, and fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues
  • To improve existing features and guide development of new ones, using anonymized data only
  • To respond to your support requests or feedback

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioral profiling, or any purpose not listed above. We do not show ads inside our apps.

04

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone. Data is shared only in the limited cases below.

Third-party service providers

Provider Purpose What is shared
Google Firebase Authentication, cloud database, file storage Account info and app content (encrypted)
Google Sign-In Optional login method Name and email only, if you choose Google login
Google Play Services App distribution and crash reporting Anonymized crash logs only

All third-party providers are bound by their own data processing agreements and privacy policies. We do not share your personal content with these providers beyond what is technically required to operate the service.

Legal disclosure

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or if necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Swift Sculpt, our users, or the public.

Business transfers

If Swift Sculpt is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via an in-app notice before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

05

Data Storage & Security

  • Data stored in the cloud is hosted on Google Firebase servers and protected with industry-standard encryption both in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Where applicable, our apps support offline mode — your data is stored locally on your device and only syncs to the cloud when you reconnect to the internet
  • Biometric authentication is processed entirely on your device by your phone's operating system — your biometric data never leaves your phone or reaches our servers
  • We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days
No internet required for core features. Where our apps support offline mode, all essential features work without a network connection. Your data is safe on your device and syncs automatically when reconnected.

Despite these measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

06

App Permissions Explained

Our apps may request the following device permissions. Each permission is only requested at the moment you try to use the feature that requires it — not on first install. Optional permissions can be revoked at any time from your device settings without affecting unrelated features.

Permission Why it may be needed Type
Internet Cloud sync, login, and online features Core
Storage / Files Save exported files (PDFs, documents) to your device Core
Notifications Reminders, alerts, and due-date notifications Optional
Camera Scanning documents, receipts, or images within the app Optional
Microphone Voice input features where available Optional
Location Location-based features where available Optional
Contacts Import contacts into the app when you choose to Optional
Biometrics On-device app lock via Face ID or fingerprint Optional

Not all permissions apply to every app. Each app only requests the permissions relevant to its own features. You can manage permissions at any time via your phone's Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Permissions.

07

Children's Privacy

Our apps are intended for users aged 13 and above (or the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at the address below. We will promptly delete that information from our records.

08

Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data at all times. Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information directly within the app or by contacting us
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data permanently — available from within the app or by contacting us
  • Portability: Export your data in a standard format where the app supports it
  • Restriction: Ask us to limit how we process your data in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data where we rely on legitimate interests
  • Opt-out: Disable push notifications, optional permissions, or any optional feature at any time without losing core app functionality

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

09

Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time as we release new apps, add new features, or as legal requirements change. When we make significant changes we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Display an in-app notice the next time you open any of our apps

Minor clarifications that do not change how your data is handled will be updated without a separate notification. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of any Swift Sculpt app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
